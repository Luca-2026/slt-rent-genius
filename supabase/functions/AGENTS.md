# Edge function rules
- Manual return reminders use claim_rental_return_reminder and send-transactional-email with server-owned recipient/branch snapshots, Berlin operational deadlines, explicit no-response confirmation and durable stage IDs; no scheduler initiates reminders, and only provider-accepted first reminders enable escalation.
- Stripe deposit payout completion requires matching succeeded durable provider receipts; reconciliation updates pending settlements only after the full balance is verified, never from a manual paid flag.
- Provider receipts accept card re_ and PayPal/local-payment pyr_ IDs with immutable facts; deposit status synchronization is read-only at Stripe, and deposit plus credit execution both persist receipts through refund-reconciliation before displaying completion.
- Offer revisions preserve a previously selected bank rebate with pure bank receipts in items, totals and payment policy; fully bank-paid orders cannot reopen checkout at the undiscounted price.
- Registered test invoice issuance completes automatic credit PDFs through test-invoice-documents and returns before customer mail; failed PDF completion remains retryable without duplicate accounting credits.
- Direct rental-credit refunds share credit_note_refunds and the original-payment cap; invoiceWithRefunds appends the current payment statement to the original invoice for opening/resending, never creates a separate refund PDF or changes taxable totals. Bank completion requires pure bank evidence and an immutable transfer reference.
- Pre-invoice rental refunds reuse creditRefund with frozen rental_refund_bases and rental_order_refunds; shared original-payment guards count all ledgers, and final invoice issuance requires confirmed deposit/rental payouts while retaining receipt allocations and explicit refund methods.
- Transfer QR amounts use document-transfer for shared offer/invoice PDFs; reserve separate text/code columns and never request payment for settled invoices or credits. Invoice refund confirmations group only confirmed payouts by their recorded method.
- New bank incentives require hasRentalPaymentChoice and the scoped offer rollout; invoice-term, sales and external flows never acquire a promise. Discount advertising belongs to offers/payment choice only; issued invoice arithmetic retains agreed rebates without new promotional terms.
- Staff bank receipts atomically select the agreed three-percent price and record actual money through record_rental_staff_payments with optimistic payment/version checks; subsequent ABs/invoices read the saved operational snapshot. Offer transfer QRs use shared rental pricing plus unchanged deposit, never total × 0.97.
- Rental incentive agreements persist from server-validated inquiries into offers; unresolved Stripe attempts retain their immutable request and idempotency claim across errors, blocking new methods until provider reconciliation to prevent duplicate payable sessions.
- Staff rental payment choices use `_shared/staff-payment-decision.ts` and the same protected RPC as public choices; failed document reads block changes so issued invoices and settlements cannot be bypassed.
- Anruf-Kundenlinks use `_shared/customer-dossier-link.ts` with the persisted CRM ID and `aktion=ansehen`; the staff-only dossier opens without edits and survives login, avoiding name-based navigation.
- AB email/PDF and new invoice evidence must honor saved offer name/description overrides rather than linked catalogue labels; AB uses offerItemText without mutating the accepted snapshot, preventing customer-facing document drift.
- Sales inquiry offers use _shared/sales-offer.ts for bank-only payment, one-time line normalization and optional informational financing; block sales at creation, acceptance, reminders and public checkout, including legacy payment links, while preserving rental payment and refund behavior.
- Sales AB amendments dispatch through buildSalesOrderAmendment and protected save_sales_order_amendment; preserve original offer evidence and append-only revisions without invoking rental availability or Stripe creation.

- Legacy B2B protocol HTML footers must use `unifiedFooterHtml` from `_shared/pdf-footer.ts`; their email PDFs use `drawUnifiedFooter` with the reservation location so both outputs match business documents.
- Online payments (Stripe) only via `_shared/stripe-pay.ts` (tested): amount = offer gross + deposit − recorded payments; the webhook books a payment into `inquiry.payments` only on an exact cent match, idempotently, so the order confirmation stays tied to full payment. Deposit refunds only via `offer-payment-admin` (admin and branch manager); in Stripe test mode only the test customer address receives a payment link.
- Fußzeile aller Geschäfts-PDFs (Angebot, Auftragsbestätigung, Rechnung, Rechnungskorrektur, Protokolle) nur über supabase/functions/_shared/pdf-footer.ts inkl. Standort der Auftragsabwicklung – eine Fußzeile, kein Auseinanderlaufen je Dokumentart.
- PDF company details come from `_shared/offer-company.ts` and `_shared/company.ts` so legal identities stay consistent.
- Route designated test-customer copies and acceptance notifications through `_shared/test-email-routing.ts` to avoid operational mailboxes.
- Stripe payment snapshots are immutable; invoices rehydrate provider receipts server-side to prevent draft edits. Credit refunds use _shared/credit-refund.ts through offer-payment-admin with persisted allocations, stable idempotency and webhook sync to prevent duplicate refunds.
- Refund inserts and updates serialize per original PaymentIntent through guard_shared_stripe_refund_capacity; capacity counts individual deposit/credit allocations, including unresolved attempts. Deposit requests persist their request ID and survive ambiguous outcomes; synchronization must validate provider identity and never regress terminal state.
- Refund webhooks persist provider facts through refund-reconciliation, including paginated charge.refunded; execution rechecks shared capacity and blocks unallocated external refunds without rewriting original documents.
- New rental offers and amendments validate calendar-day machine insurance and explicit deductible through insurance-validation; legacy issued invoices remain evidence, never automatically converted.
- Fonio uses phone_calls, fonio-call-webhook and tested _shared/callPriority.ts for consistent lists/counters; fonio-live-lookup never books or uses AI. call-daily-digest runs at 20:00 Berlin with call_digest_log deduplication. Anhänger und Halteverbotsschilder-Sets sind online-only: liveLookup.ts kennzeichnet sie über onlineBookingOnly mit Website-Verweis (keine telefonische Aufnahme/Bestätigung), phoneCallAnalysis.ts wertet Verweis-Anrufe als info ohne open_points.
- Zeitgesteuerte Edge Functions (Cron) prüfen den Header x-cron-secret gegen public.internal_cron_secret (nur service_role) über _shared/cronAuth.ts – pg_cron liest den Wert zur Laufzeit, damit kein Fremdaufruf Mails oder interne Daten auslöst.
- Unaccepted offer followups share payment-reminder's existing hourly invocation; offer_link_deadline caps future rentals at snapshot start, but freshly sent already-started rentals at snapshot end and Berlin validity, so same-day offers are not born expired. Decision triggers serialize acceptance/decline and never reopen terminal links; offer_followup_deliveries plus provider idempotency track dispatch. offer_followup_settings.live_enabled gates production rollout; test calls require the designated authenticated test customer and labelled test inquiry.
- Paid sign bookings: sign-booking-worker sends one location-mailbox notice (staff_notified_at, permit attached) and sync_sign_booking_tasks creates a task for both packages.
- Offer acceptance rules: see offer-accept/AGENTS.md. Renty chat rules: see public-chat/AGENTS.md. Shared helper rules: see _shared/AGENTS.md.
