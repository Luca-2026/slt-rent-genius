# Shared helper rules
- Stale staff payment snapshots return PT409 instead of 40001; optimistic validation must reject once, not trigger database API serialization retry storms.
- Staff bank receipts preserve issued invoice/settlement prices instead of implicitly requesting a rebate switch; document-read failures block recording to prevent price drift.
 - Weekly rental tariffs use tierPriceWeek in both rental-pricing mirrors, counting elapsed 24-hour blocks without Bau weekend exclusions; weekly offers serialize one flat amount and explicit period labels, while nextInstallmentDate preserves monthly plans unless a day interval is saved.
- Delivery tariff bands are net; cartDeliveryQuote adds VAT once while website calculators use shared customer-type display, keeping new inquiry quotes consistent without rewriting issued documents.
- Stripe receipt invoice reconciliation uses stripe-invoice-sync on first and duplicate webhook deliveries; committed order receipts must not suppress retries after a failed invoice allocation.
- Optional per-line delivery_at/collection_at are local Berlin moments normalized by _shared/offer-logistics and printed through calendarDescription; offerInsurancePeriod uses them for inclusive insurance days with per-endpoint rental-calendar fallback. Rental billing and availability stay calendar-based; manual insurance overrides and issued snapshots are preserved.
- Open-ended rentals share unit/minimum-duration normalization in _shared/installments.ts; manual inquiries keep these in raw_payload, offers in immutable snapshots, and day-based rates must never be inferred as monthly installment amounts.
- New open-ended offers persist independent-pricing metadata: per-line rate/unit/count stay separate from contractual minimum duration. Legacy snapshots retain their old semantics until explicitly switched; tariff unit changes require a new rate, and maintained daily tiers are never silently relabeled as weekly/monthly/yearly rates.
- Explicit undated_offer quotes persist in inquiry raw_payload, offer drafts and snapshots; duration/unit remain independently editable per line, calendars/availability are omitted, and no operational dates are invented. Dated and open-ended rentals retain their existing rules.
- Rückgabe an anderem Standort (One-Way) nur über das Rückgabefeld im InquiryOfferForm + resolveReturnLocation (_shared/inquiry-offer-math.ts); Aufpreis netto als eigene Position, gespeichert im offer_payload und inquiry_invoices.return_location/return_location_cost – Angebot, Rechnung, Auftragsbestätigung und Umsatz rechnen so dieselbe Summe.
- Paket-4-Angebotsteile (Ihre Daten, Anrede, Auftragsart, Gruppen, Dauer-Spalte, Legende, Angebotsbedingungen) nur über generateOfferPdf({ inquiryOffer }) für Mietanfrage-Angebote – Rechnungen, Gutschriften, AB und Portal-Angebote bleiben unverändert. Bedingungen aus text_blocks "offer_terms" via _shared/offer-terms.ts: ab Absatz 2 nur bei Bau-Artikel.
- Inquiry rejections decide per requested article via _shared/inquiry-rejection.ts: the server reads items from the stored inquiry, the browser sends only index-based available/alternative decisions, at least one item must be unavailable, and the email lists every article with status in the customer's address form — so multi-item requests are never reduced to the first article.
