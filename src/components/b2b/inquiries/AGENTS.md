# Inquiries
- RentalCancellationDialog persists a server-validated fee snapshot before offering invoice preview/send; individual fees replace the AGB fee, original contracts remain unchanged, and existing-invoice cases require explicit correction to avoid duplicate payment claims.
- Event tariff amounts are flat per article, with persisted inline period decisions blocking dispatch; normalization and reopening retain event basis so quantities never multiply calendar days again.
 - Weekly tariffs use elapsed 24-hour blocks with full started weeks and serialize as one period-labelled flat line; manual period/unit changes require an explicit price decision, and four-week installments use an additive day interval without changing historical monthly schedules.
- Offer time selectors share quarter-hour options and preserve legacy values until edited; appended positions inherit the final group, and catalogue selections retain valid groups so new lines stay last through grouping and serialization.
- Invoice editors inherit payment_terms and custom conditions from the operational offer snapshot alongside customer references; explicit draft/staff choices remain visible and are not silently replaced.
- Unchanged offer resends use ResendOfferButton and resend-inquiry-offer with server-owned recipient confirmation, existing PDF and acceptance link; this prevents revisions or new document numbers for address corrections.
- Deposit refund controls reconcile provider evidence before execution and resume unresolved durable requests; depositRefundPayment only chooses new unreserved receipts. Pre-invoice rental refunds use an explicitly prepared server-calculated preview basis; final invoice controls stay blocked until confirmed payouts, never a second claim for reserved capacity.
- Invoice service-period defaults use invoiceServicePeriod to inherit agreed rental item dates with inquiry fallback; nonempty manual dates survive draft restoration, while empty legacy drafts and reset regain the rental default.
- Online-link status must defer to saved receipts (bank or cash) and the current order balance; fully covered orders never expose another payment link, and a stale active link never blocks AB amendments, merely because a historical link remains active.
- Setup/dismantle controls edit period-free offer lines with unit descriptions and fractional quantities; legacy scalar costs migrate once and are zeroed to avoid double billing. Outbound and return transport use independent choices, persisted return_mode, and only selected legs retain costs.
- Rental document workspaces start in a read-only order summary with a visible position toggle; first opening an accepted order selects amendment, reopening retains the selected mode/draft. Editors mount only after explicit actions and close on successful completion; amendment saves still advance to AB review/send before closing.
- Period-free service lines preserve unit/VAT and omit dates and inventory checks; snapshots carry period_free through reopening and downstream documents.
- Rental machine insurance uses full-calendar-period pricing by default; per-item addon labels and deductible notes remain editable snapshot fields, never catalogue mutations, so the PDF preserves agreed insurance terms.
- Persisted optional insurance tiers use the same editor as standard addons; missing current CMS configuration must not delete them, and manual edits must survive tier synchronization.
- Editor bank rebates are recalculated through editorBankDiscount after manual price/discount edits; server billing quantity is converted once. The retired AdditionalPayout compensation route must never be exposed; deposit and rental refunds use the original payment with a shared cumulative cap, preserving original receipts.
- AB editing enters the existing amendment editor, never offer revision; normal AB sends require a preview matching note, attachment choice, payments and operational snapshot, and preview must not reserve devices or send mail.
- Invoice references inherit offer extra_fields with editor overrides for rental/sales and all invoice kinds; issued PDFs remain immutable.
- Sales offer mode uses shared sales-offer policy for one-time quantities, bank-only payment and optional informational financing stored in drafts/snapshots; rental and invoice modes retain their existing paths to avoid business-type drift.
- Rental offer weekend-use defaults follow explicit per-line request/snapshot flags before global flags; staff checkbox changes update that flag on every line and normalization preserves it, so duration, tier pricing and reopening cannot disagree.
- Rental offer calendar fields are the authoritative period; offerCalendarPeriod derives descriptions and default duration. Explicit duration_override controls billed duration and tier selection only, persists in snapshots, and never changes calendar-based insurance or availability; clearing it restores calendar calculation. Inherited lines keep their own billing unit; issued snapshots remain unchanged.
- Order amendments reuse InquiryOfferForm in amendment mode with sales/rental policy dispatch; protected save_sales_order_amendment/save_rental_order_amendment preserve original_accepted_offer and append-only business-specific revisions. Sales never create rental dates, reservations or online payment links. New documents read operational snapshots; issued invoice snapshots never change.
- Sign setup bookings create one idempotent checklist per booking: permit review before paid setup; expired holds close tasks. Private uploads are verified before acceptance; additional signs use CMS configuration, are catalog/location-validated inside atomic reservation, and never add a deposit.
- Sign calendars and reservations share sign_reserved_sets; setup distance is resolved by the trusted booking function, never client input. ProductPriceBlock owns every rental price layout and customer switch, including packages.
- Invoice and installment editors require a matching current PDF preview before final sending; send-inquiry-invoice preview uses the final renderer but returns before number allocation, storage, invoices, billing markers, schedule changes and emails.
- Angebots-Paket 3b: Gruppen, Zusatzfelder, Auftragsart, Mengeneinheit, Overrides und optionale Versicherungsstufen nur als Felder im offer_payload (Helfer in offerMath.ts / _shared/inquiry-offer-math.ts, getestet); optionale Addons (optional = true) zählen nirgends in Summen und entfallen in Rechnungen – alte Angebote ohne die Felder bleiben gültig. Zusatzfelder (Besteller, Kostenstelle, Bauvorhaben) stehen im PDF-Infoblock. AdminCreateOfferDialog.tsx kennt Gruppen, Auftragsart und Zusatzfelder noch nicht.
- InquiryOfferForm gliedert sich in nummerierte EditorSection-Blöcke (1 Positionen, 2 Logistik, 3 Kaution/Zahlung, 4 Angaben/Hinweis, dann Summe) – neue Felder in den passenden Block statt als lose Kachel, damit Reihenfolge und Fluchtlinien einheitlich bleiben.
- offerPayloadToLines liest articles/duration/unit_code, wenn vorhanden (quantity = Stück × Dauer); splitQuantity nur für Altangebote.
- Preserve Miettag/Miettage when restoring legacy offer snapshots; catalog selections must include the CMS pricing scheme and base tiers so Bau defaults do not fall back to calendar days.

- Both rental offer forms use offerDeposit/OfferDepositWarning; require acknowledgement before finalizing without a deposit, not for drafts.
- Angebots-/Rechnungsentwürfe im Anfragenbereich zusätzlich in inquiry_doc_drafts (Server, je draft_key) speichern und nach Versand/Verwerfen löschen – Kollegen sehen begonnene Arbeit, nichts geht verloren oder wird doppelt erfasst.
- Mietanfragen: Vollseite /b2b/mietvorgaenge/:id (InquiryDetailPanel layout="page"), ?anfrage= leitet nur um; Verkauf bleibt gestapelt.
- Zahlungseingänge (payments jsonb) stempelt der DB-Trigger stamp_inquiry_payments mit Erfasser/Zeitpunkt und protokolliert in inquiry_payment_events – Browser kann Erfasser nicht fälschen; Clients müssen recorded_* unverändert zurückschreiben.
- Kautionsabrechnung nur über DepositSettlementCard + src/lib/depositSettlement.ts (getestet) und DB-Funktion settle_deposit (rechnet serverseitig nach, 409 bei Abweichung, danach unveränderlich); Abzüge als Rechnung mit deposit_settlement-Flag, damit Mietzahlungen nicht doppelt angerechnet werden. Rückgabekosten sind Rechnungspositionen in zwei Kategorien: Service (Reinigung, Tanken, Mehrstunden) mit 19 % USt, Schadensersatz (Schäden am Mietgerät) ohne USt – vat_rate je Position (inquiry_invoice_items.vat_rate, 0 oder 19), Werkstattleistungen an Kundenmaschinen bleiben normale 19-%-Leistungen.
- Deposit allocation caps at the explicit offer snapshot deposit and preserves excess rent payments as rental_credit for invoice evidence; missing legacy values retain historical allocation, and issued settlements must never be rewritten. Final-invoice payments come from stored receipts, not clipped browser drafts.
- Rechnungsversand ist gesperrt, solange eine Kaution (Zahlung über Miete) nicht abgerechnet ist (useDepositState/depositBlocksInvoice, getestet); ohne Kaution übernimmt die Rechnung unberechnete Rückgabekosten, nach Abrechnung zählen Zahlungen nur bis zur Miete.
